## Honesty notes

- The "36k/s wall" chase is documented end to end (§14.1–14.8): six client
  levers were neutral, the delivered-vs-ledger denominators were untangled
  (decoded == collapsed == delivered; the gap was pre-slot events covered by
  the bootstrap snapshot), and the number that matters is DELIVERED
  changes/s, which apitap consumes 100% of.
- L1a (zero-fill removal, advance) measured neutral within noise and is kept
  without a win claim (§13.6).
