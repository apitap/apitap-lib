# Deep review 3 — v0.63.0, fixes audited, every slice re-read

Read-only review of tag `v0.63.0` (`11397c4`). 8 fix auditors checked the 38 commits since `c216373` (the fixes for deep reviews 1 and 2); 11 slice reviewers re-read ~76k lines of `crates/apitap-core` and `py-apitap`; every P0/P1 was attacked by two verifiers (code-path, reachability). CONFIRMED = both upheld. 87 agents, ~11M subagent tokens over two runs (the first was interrupted and resumed from cache). Nothing was built or run: each finding needs a RED leg on the rig before its fix. Known items from `2026-10-20-deep-review.md` and `2026-10-20-deep-review-2.md` are not repeated.

Commits after the reviewed tag: `b19badc` (D15 binary session falls back to text on a historic type) and `72a4605` (APITAP_GITHUB_API); neither touches a finding below except P2 github_api Link guard, which names 72a4605.

Duplicates (independent reviewers hit the same defect): the per-table order_by dict leak into log_based bootstraps (3 reports), the ch->ch Nullable relay framing (3), the N24 GIPK self-refusal (2), the N24 implicit TIMESTAMP default (2), the N4 max_error_count wedge (2), the MariaDB RENAME IF EXISTS/WAIT miss (2). About 22 distinct defects.
